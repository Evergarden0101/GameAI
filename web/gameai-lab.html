Tactical squad AI · line-of-sight · cover · suppression

Fire and maneuver

The core loop of shooter squad AI from Doom to F.E.A.R.: the anchor pins the player with suppressing fire so they tunnel-vision on the visible threat, while the flanker takes a concealed path to a new angle for the kill.

TACTICS.SIMt=0
Squad advancing…

Controls

Player — a dug-in defender. Return fire is strong… until suppression pins them.
Anchor — moves to a firing position and suppresses, drawing the player's attention.
Flanker — hugs cover (dashed path) to reach an unguarded bearing, then fires.
Walls — hard cover that blocks movement and line-of-sight.
Adversarial search · minimax + alpha-beta

The opponent that cannot lose

This board searches the entire game tree with minimax and plays perfectly. The best you can do is force a draw — which is the whole point of the algorithm behind Deep Blue. Go ahead, try to win.

MINIMAX.SIMnodes: —
You are X. Your move.

Controls

Each time the AI moves it runs minimax: it assumes you'll play optimally, scores every reachable end-state, and picks the move with the best guaranteed outcome. Alpha-beta pruning skips branches that can't change the choice — the nodes counter shows how many positions it actually evaluated.

Result over many games against any opponent: it never loses. Perfect play from both sides is always a draw.
