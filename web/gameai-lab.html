Adversarial search · minimax + alpha-beta

Try to beat the search

On Perfect, this board searches the whole game tree and cannot lose — the best you can force is a draw. Drop it to Casual and it plays loose, so you can actually win. Your move.

MINIMAX.SIMnodes: —
You are X. Your move.

✊ Click a square to play X. The AI replies as O.

Each AI move runs minimax with alpha-beta pruning; the nodes counter is how many positions it evaluated. On Perfect it is unbeatable — perfect play from both sides is always a draw.

Movement & navigation · A\* pathfinding

Move the goal, build the maze

Click to move the goal; the pursuer re-runs A* every step to reach it. Switch to Walls and draw your own maze — watch it re-route instantly. Faint cells are the search frontier; the bright line is the shortest path.

ASTAR.SIMexpanded: 0
Click to place the goal.

🧭 Tap to move the goal · switch to Walls to draw obstacles.

A* expands the frontier cell with the best f = g + h — distance travelled plus an estimate of distance remaining. The expanded count is how many cells it touched for each path.

Learning · reinforcement learning

Set the goal, watch it learn

Click any cell to move the goal (or the pit) and the agent relearns from scratch. Tabular Q-learning trains live: the value map lights up and the policy arrows snap into a route to your goal — from reward alone, no map given.

QLEARN.SIMepisode 0
Click a cell to move the goal.

🧠 Tap a cell to place the goal; toggle to move the pit.

Each cell's shade is its learned value max Q(s,a) (brighter = closer to reward); the arrows are the greedy policy. Move the goal and watch a fresh route converge in a few hundred episodes.
